## What "the data" is

- the records of the runs: every file on the `benchmark-data` branch;
- the figures computed from them: `summary.json` and the tables and charts of the results
  site;
- the list of searches in `queries/`;
- the declared prices in `providers/*/provider.yaml`, as a compilation. The prices
  themselves are facts published by each provider on its own pricing page.

The code is not data: it is under the MIT licence, see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## How to give credit

Name the source and link to it, and say so if you changed the figures:

> Source: ScrapeBeaver SERP API Benchmark, https://benchmark.scrapebeaver.com (CC BY 4.0)

When quoting a figure, give the date it was read: the results change with every run.

## What the licence does not cover

Provider names and logos belong to their owners and are used here only to say whose
service was measured. The data comes without warranty: see the methodology for how each
number is computed and how sure it is.
